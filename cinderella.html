About the Story

Cinderella is a famous fairy tale about a kind young girl who lives with her stepmother and stepsisters. She is treated badly but remains kind and hopeful.

With the help of her fairy godmother, Cinderella attends a royal ball and meets the prince. Her story ends happily when the prince finds her using the glass slipper.
